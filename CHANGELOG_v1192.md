# v1.19.2 — Japanese-first learning share

- Home now has a dedicated `POKER STUDY / 今日の学び` section.
- GTO Wizard articles prioritize the official Japanese GTO Wizard feed.
- English-only article rows and GTO Wizard news/video-index posts do not occupy the article list.
- YouTube is restricted to a curated source allowlist rather than arbitrary search results.
- Trusted video sources: GTO Wizard Japan, ヨコサワポーカーチャンネル, POKER BROTHERS.
- Video recommendations are separated into strategy and motivation categories.
- External feeds use short timeouts, a six-hour cache, and stale-while-revalidate so the home screen is never blocked by an external site.
- Verified Japanese articles/videos remain available as fallback content when external feeds fail.
- External fetch targets are HTTPS allowlisted to prevent user-controlled server-side requests.
